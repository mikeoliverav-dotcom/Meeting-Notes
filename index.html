How it works

  1. Record. Click Start recording and choose what to share. For calls in a browser (Meet, Zoom web), pick the tab and leave “Share tab audio” on. For the Teams or Zoom desktop apps, pick Entire screen and turn on “Share system audio.” Your microphone is added too.
  2. Capture. Click Screenshot (or press Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V here) and type quick notes. Everything is time-stamped.
  3. Transcribe. After the call, click Transcribe. The speech model downloads the first time and is then saved in your browser.
  4. Summarize. Copy for Claude, paste Claude's reply back, and download a PDF or Word file with your screenshots in place.
